# Code of Conduct

MCflare is a small hobby project. Keep project spaces useful, technical, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and bugs without attacking the people involved.
- Give enough technical context for others to reproduce or understand a problem.
- Accept that maintainers may decline features that add complexity outside MCflare's scope.
- Respect privacy. Redact player addresses, credentials, account/session tokens, and unrelated production data.
- Credit prior work and licenses when reusing code or research.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, credential/data exposure, or repeatedly ignoring project-scope and safety boundaries is not acceptable in issues, pull requests, reviews, or other MCflare project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and usable. Security-sensitive material should be reported through the private path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not posted publicly.
